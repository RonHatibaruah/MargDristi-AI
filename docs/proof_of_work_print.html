SECTION B: 16:9 TECHNOLOGICAL FRAMEWORK INFOGRAPHICS SUITE ARCHITECTURAL BLUEPRINTS

The following 6 high-impact architectural blueprints illustrate the complete end-to-end technological framework of MargDristi AI, formatted in 16:9 widescreen engineering style:

Blueprint 1: Operational Paradigm Shift — Comparing Traditional Subjective Windshield Survey vs. MargDristi AI Closed-Loop Execution

Technical Annotation: Demonstrates the replacement of manual windshield observation (5 km/day, ₹1,200/km, weeks of processing) with vehicle-mounted edge intelligence (60 km/h, <₹45/km, instant PostGIS synchronization, and cryptographic repair validation).

Blueprint 2: Edge-to-Cloud Distributed System Architecture & Multi-Tier Data Pipeline

Technical Annotation: Delineates the 4 architectural tiers: (1) Edge Inference & DPDP Scrubbing Tier; (2) Cloud Ingestion & PostGIS Spatial Database; (3) Analytics & Decision Engine (ASTM D6433 + IRC:82); (4) Web GIS Presentation & Automated Work Order Dispatch Tier.

Blueprint 3: Pavement Distress Taxonomy & ASTM D6433 Pavement Condition Index (PCI) Architecture

Technical Annotation: Defines the 6 standardized distress categories (Potholes, Alligator Cracks, Longitudinal/Transverse, Rutting, Ravelling, Edge Breaks), their deduct weight curves, and the mathematical formula for computing 100m segment PCI scores.

Blueprint 4: Closed-Loop Governance & Role-Based Operational Workflow across Assam PWD Tiers

Technical Annotation: Details the handoffs between Junior Engineer (Surveyor), Assistant Executive Engineer (Validation), Executive Engineer (Sanction & BoQ), and Contractor (GPS-tagged post-repair upload and AI before/after matching).

Blueprint 5: Multi-Criteria Algorithmic Prioritisation Matrix Aligned with IRC:82-2015

Technical Annotation: Illustrates the multi-attribute utility calculation synthesizing Segment Distress Score (40%), Traffic Intensity / Commercial Vehicles (25%), Monsoon & Flood Vulnerability (20%), and Strategic Freight Connectivity (15%) to allocate maintenance budgets.

Blueprint 6: 9-Month Stage-Gate Development Roadmap, Phase Deliverables & Gateway Criteria
